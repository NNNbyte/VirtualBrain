# Study-002 dossier: virtual brain twins for treatment choice (Wang et al. 2025 virtual patients)

**Status:** exploratory. Research on virtual (simulated) patients; not a clinical recommendation. Revision 2 after RV reviews A0047 and A0050.

## Answer to the study question
- **EZ recovery on the blinded test patient (P2): not achieved.**
  - The inversion pipeline was not qualified. v2 benchmark A0037: arm A x0 R-hat max 4.73 with the planted EZ ranked 2nd; arm B (mismatched connectome) R-hat max 4.91 with the EZ ranked 6th. v3 benchmark A0040 (Pathfinder approximations; NUTS never finished warm-up): arm A (matching connectome) P(EZ rank 1) 0.012; arm B (mismatched connectome) EZ rank 28 by mean, P(rank 1) 0.205.
  - R5 gate verdicts (A0042): failed for A0014, A0018, A0019, A0037, A0039, A0040; indeterminate for A0017 and for SimulationBatch A0038 (numerical check not clean, see below); passed for A0034, A0035.
  - The frozen exploratory P2 estimates (A0017-A0019) missed the true single-region EZ (Left-F1-lateral-prefrontal): recall 0 in all four estimates; true-EZ ranks 159, 46, 133 and 62 of 162 (A0043). A0043 notes the true EZ appeared only as a propagation candidate of hypothesis H1.
- **Best virtual intervention: not determinable.**
  - Primary (post-stimulation) window: no model sample seized at baseline, so the treatment ranking there is uninformative (A0038).
  - Full window (sensitivity; 12 of 24 MAP optima, prespecified by log density): ablation of the estimated EZ + PZ reduced seizing regions most (median -3.0, 5-95% -4.9 to -1.55; all seizures abolished in 5 of 12 samples, 41.7%); sustained-current SEEG stimulation -0.5 to -1.0; pulse-train arms that ran cleanly had median 0 (A0038).
  - Numerical failures (non-finite runs, excluded from effects; A0038): full window (12 samples): STIM-PT amp 2: 2/12, amp 4: 9/12; STIM-PT-chronic amp 2: 10/12, amp 4: 12/12. Post window (24 samples): STIM-PT amp 4: 24/24; STIM-PT-chronic amp 4: 23/24.
  - The fitted model's baseline did not reproduce the recording: 0 of the 10 most active simulated channels overlapped with the recording's top 10, in both windows (A0038).
  - R5 reran one model sample x 6 arms with the same simulator and matched them bit-exactly (A0041); the other runs were not independently rerun.
  - On the true model the virtual patient did not seize spontaneously in the region-level simulator (0 seizing regions), so no candidate, including ablating the true EZ, could show an effect; rank concordance is undefined, and full-window sign agreement was 9/18 with all 9 predicted reductions wrong (A0043).

## Why it failed (lessons)
1. Model mismatch: data generated by the authors' richer (6D / neural-field) model are not reproduced by the 2D Epileptor with a linear log-power observation map; fewer parameters, better priors and other samplers did not fix this (A0037, A0040).
2. Paradigm mismatch: P2's seizure is stimulation-induced and short (seizure-like activity about 3.5-7.4 s, about 4 s, per A0009), but the inversion had no stimulation input and few observations, and the true model does not seize spontaneously; treatments must be evaluated under the observed stimulation protocol (not done; next step).
3. Controls that worked: benchmarks exposed the failure before any claim; the truth stayed sealed until predictions were frozen (freeze at ledger seq 205, reveal at seq 214); R5's spot reruns matched bit-exactly.

## Next steps
- Simulation-based inference with the full model (scan candidate EZs by simulating the observed stimulation protocol and matching SEEG features), benchmarked first on synthetic data.
- Re-evaluate treatments under the observed stimulation protocol, with neural-field simulation using the patient anatomy (data/wang-co2-ana) and TI mapped via the ico5 region map (need N001).
