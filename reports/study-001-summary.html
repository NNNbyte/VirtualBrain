For clinicians

  • Resectable focus, especially mesial temporal lobe epilepsy: open temporal resection has the strongest evidence and is the only option with randomised evidence against continued medication. 58–73% were free of disabling seizures at 1–2 years, and 66–72.5% were seizure-free long term.
  • LITT: 53–58% Engel I at 12–24 months, with fewer complications than open surgery. The gap to open surgery is not significant after matching in one pooled analysis, and there is no RCT.
  • Neurostimulation (RNS, ANT-DBS, VNS) is palliative. Median seizure reduction is about 40–75%, up to 82% in one RNS cohort, but seizure freedom is uncommon (8–22%). No head-to-head trial compares the modalities.
  • Model-guided ("virtual brain twin") stimulation has no clinical outcome evidence yet.

For engineers

  • A deterministic controller admitted 33 tasks for 6 agent roles. It refused 3 proposals and recorded 6 repairs. Outcome labels were sealed in a vault that was never opened.
  • The Wang 2025 vep-stim inversion pipeline failed both qualification benchmarks. Sampling did not converge (R-hat up to 4.24) and the planted or documented seizure-onset zone was not reliably recovered, even with the true connectome.
  • R5 traced the failure to the method: amplitude vs EZ-extent non-identifiability, priors that conflict with realistic feature levels, an iid likelihood on autocorrelated noise, and a multimodal posterior. Three package defects were also found and patched in a copy.
  • So no patient was modeled, and intervention design and outcome evaluation (P4–P5) were not run.

6 · Virtual-patient modeling (simulation and inference)

The planned method was Wang et al. 2025's virtual brain twin (vep-stim, commit 5367ef4). It fits a 162-region 2D Epileptor network to SEEG features with Stan, and the region excitability x0 above about −2.05 marks a region as seizing (the epileptogenic zone, EZ). Before fitting any patient, the pipeline had to pass two benchmarks where the right answer is known.

Verdict (A0098)

✕ not qualified The pipeline, as packaged or as patched, is not qualified for fitting HUP patients with a template connectome. The failures lie mainly in the method (identifiability, priors, observation model), not in run length. Patient modeling, intervention simulation (P4) and outcome evaluation (P5) were therefore not run. A virtual-patient result, had there been one, would be a hypothesis about a model, not a clinical prediction.

7 · Quality controls and traceability

Outcome blinding

  • 9 outcome-bearing files were sealed in the vault (V001–V009) and their plaintext deleted.
  • The vault is readable only by an R5 outcome-evaluation task after predictions are frozen. Predictions were never frozen and the vault was never opened (0 access events).
  • Disclosed exposure: before sealing, discovery read some participants files, so dataset-level outcome aggregates appear in superseded records (A0033, A0058) and in three cards. No per-patient labels are on record outside the vault. Any future outcome evaluation on this split must report this.

Independent checking

  • 101 gate decisions; the controller refuses a gate by the producing role.
  • R5 re-derived every number it gated (source passages, file hashes, R-hat from raw draws). RV reviewed the dossier, found 7 issues and accepted the repaired version.
  • 6 repairs marked 28 artifacts stale until replaced; 3 task proposals were refused at admission.

8 · Limitations and next steps

Limitations

  • All literature evidence is abstract-level: late-timepoint denominators and responder definitions are unverified (needs N002, N003).
  • VNS pooled and registry data include generalised epilepsy, which is outside the protocol population.
  • Cross-modality comparisons are indirect; resection and neurostimulation answer different questions (cure vs palliation) in different populations.
  • No virtual-patient evidence: the benchmarks used one planted EZ and one seed, and the synthetic data bypassed the feature pipeline.
  • Only 10 of 56 eligible cases were curated, within the role budgets.

Already applied for the next release

  • Controller: a summary may cite records from its own task; review reports and decisions are no longer marked stale when what they judged is repaired (2 new invariant tests).
  • R3 skill: a mandatory synthetic qualification benchmark through the full feature pipeline, per-file sampling rate, and observation-model checks.
  • R1 skill: never read outcome-bearing files; dataset cards hold field names only.
  • Lead skill: stop modeling on a failed benchmark; check budgets before modeling. R3 budget raised 10 → 14 and R5 10 → 12.
  • To revisit patient modeling: a pipeline that passes the benchmark (need N014), e.g. a reparameterised likelihood with AR(1) noise and data-centred priors, plus an MNI template connectome.
